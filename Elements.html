Fact 1: The amazing Octopus


Octopuses have 3 hearts, 9 brains and blue blood. 2 hearts pump blood to the gills while the third heart circulates blood to the rest of the body.


How smart are Octopuses?


They are not just smart, they are some of the smartest invertebrates on the planet! Octopuses can use tools, solve mazes, and even open jars.
